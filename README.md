## Research

### Tor and Network Analysis

I have worked on research involving Tor infrastructure, network traffic analysis, and probabilistic correlation techniques.

**TOR–UNVEIL**

A research project exploring probabilistic Tor traffic analysis through network observations, traffic characteristics, relay information, circuit feasibility, and statistical correlation.

**D-Anon**

A security research platform for analyzing Tor infrastructure and relationships between observed services and infrastructure.

The objective of this work is to support security investigations through explainable analysis rather than treating network attribution as a simple identification problem.
